# Labs

Labs are **experimental and fast moving models** available for a limited amount of time.  
We provide access to these models for developers and the community to try them out and provide feedback, allowing us to iterate faster and improve them!

:::note
If you are interested in our Labs models for enterprise use cases, please [reach out to us](https://mistral.ai/contact)
:::

<SectionTab as="h1" sectionId="available-models">Available Models</SectionTab>

Labs are identified by the `Labs` tag in the model card and the `labs-` prefix in the model name identifier.  
For example: `labs-devstral-small-2512`

As lab models, we iterate and may update them frequently. They are subject to change and **may be deprecated or removed with 1 month's notice.** See the [model lifecycle policy](/inference/model-lifecycle) for details on all model stages.
