- We released the **Voxtral 3** audio family, our next generation of speech-to-text and text-to-speech models. All four models are in Public Preview. See the [Voxtral 3 announcement](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/) and the [audio models pricing](/inference/pricing#audio-models). <Tag model/>
  - **Voxtral TTS 3** (`voxtral-mini-tts-3-0`): text-to-speech with zero-shot voice cloning, streaming, and contextual delivery. See the [model card](/models/voxtral-mini-tts-3-0).
  - **Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3** (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-3-0`): offline transcription and diarization with timestamps. See the [model card](/models/voxtral-mini-transcribe-3-0).
  - **Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3** (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-3-0`): real-time transcription. See the [model card](/models/voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-3-0).
  - **Voxtral Small Transcribe 3** (`voxtral-small-transcribe-3-0`): offline transcription and diarization with timestamps. See the [model card](/models/voxtral-small-transcribe-3-0).
- We released **Voice Agents**, a managed real-time bidirectional voice pipeline (microphone capture, STT, LLM, TTS, playback) exposed through the Voice Agents SDK, with telephony, browser, and custom-audio interfaces. See the [Voice Agents overview](/studio/audio/voice-agents/overview) and [Voice Agents pricing](/inference/pricing#voice-agents). <Tag other/>
